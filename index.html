Privacy Policy for Blossom

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Blossom is a women's health and menstrual-cycle tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Blossom.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, Blossom may process information you enter into the app, such as period start and end dates, menstrual-cycle information, mood selections, and preferences used to personalize the app experience.

Health and sensitive information

Menstrual-cycle and related health information can be sensitive. Blossom uses this information only to provide app features such as cycle tracking, phase information, period estimates, and related insights. Blossom does not use health information for advertising.

Pregnancy and cycle estimates

Any fertility, ovulation, pregnancy-chance, cycle-phase, or period information shown by Blossom is an estimate based on information available to the app. It is provided for general informational purposes and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or a substitute for professional medical care. Blossom should not be relied upon as a method of contraception.

Data sharing and sale

Blossom does not sell personal or health information. Blossom does not share personal health information with advertisers.

Data storage and security

Blossom is designed to limit collection to information needed for its features. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed to provide Blossom's features or meet applicable requirements. Where the app provides controls to remove or reset information, users may use those controls to delete their stored app information. If additional assistance is required, users may contact the developer using the contact information shown on Blossom's Google Play listing.

Children

Blossom is not intended for children under 13. The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party services

If Blossom uses platform or third-party services, those services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices materially change.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Blossom evolves. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Blossom, please use the developer contact information provided on Blossom's Google Play listing.
